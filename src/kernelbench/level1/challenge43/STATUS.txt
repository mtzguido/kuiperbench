STATUS — KernelBench L1 #43 MaxPool3D
=====================================

The challenge has a complete verified implementation.  The public extracted
entry owns the W, H, and D max-pool passes, the zero-copy verified layout
recasts between them, all intermediate allocations and frees, and the final
row-major allocation.  Its postcondition is the exact nested f32 [fmax]
computation linked to the original input.

The C++ entrypoint validates the tensor metadata and SizeT/launch refinements,
selects the input CUDA device, calls that entry once, and wraps the returned
cudaMalloc pointer.  No pooling pass, permutation, allocation, or multi-kernel
orchestration remains on the host side.

The canonical challenge dimensions are (16,32,128,128,128), with kernel 3,
stride 2, padding 1, and dilation 3, producing (16,32,62,62,62).
