SKEPTIC — KernelBench L1 #40 LayerNorm
======================================

Spec faithfulness
-----------------
The verified entry point establishes a direct real-valued contract.  For
every batch row, [layernorm_post] relates each concrete output via [%~] to

  mean = (1/n) Σ x[j]
  var  = (1/n) Σ x[j]^2 - mean^2
  y[j] = ((x[j] - mean) / sqrt(var + eps)) * gamma[j] + beta[j].

There are no numerical existentials in the public row or tensor
postcondition.  Float reductions and affine intermediates remain proof-local.
The entry point computes 1/n internally and proves its real approximation;
the bridge does not supply it.

The public [layernorm_domain] precondition requires every real
[var + eps] to be positive.  On that domain, the temporary
[Kuiper.KB.Compat.RsqrtApprox.rsqrt_approx] assumption connects the float
[rsqrt] operation to [FStar.Math.Sqrt.rsqrt].  This compatibility law is
proposed for upstream Kuiper and is the numerical proof's explicit local
trust debt.

Proof shape
-----------
Two verified reductions obtain the row's sum and sum of squares.  Scalar
approximation laws derive the real normalization, and the pointwise proof
also anchors gamma and beta through [to_real_seq].  The host loop preserves
completed output rows and the untouched input suffix.

Inherited debt
--------------
[Kuiper.Kernel.Map.map_gpu2] uses [magic ()] for its kernel descriptor's
sendability fields, following the existing plain-kernel pattern.  The
LayerNorm-specific numerical proof adds no further magic or admit, but it
does depend on the local rsqrt compatibility assumption described above.

Edge cases
----------
- [b] and [n] are positive by [szp].
- A constant row with positive eps is in-domain and produces beta in real
  semantics before floating approximation.
- A row with non-positive real [var + eps] is outside the theorem's domain;
  the prior claim that an unconstrained existential covered rsqrt(0) has
  intentionally been removed.
