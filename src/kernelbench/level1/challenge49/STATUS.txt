STATUS — KernelBench L1 #49 Max reduction over dim=1 (DEFERRED)
================================================================

Reference
---------
torch.max(x, dim=1)[0]  on shape (128, 4096, 4095) → (128, 4095).

Why deferred
------------
This challenge is structurally identical to #47 (sum) and #48 (mean) at
the layout / per-row level, but the device-side primitive
[Kuiper.Kernel.HReduce.Block.reduce_batched_block] is hard-coded to the
addition monoid (zero, +.) at every level of its 706-line proof.

  src/lib/kernel/Kuiper.Kernel.HReduce.Block.fst:117,129,145,147,217,
                                                341,351,458,470,476,
                                                482,554,560,...

Generalising it to an arbitrary commutative monoid (m, ⊕, e) — which
is what is needed to instantiate at (f32, fmax, -∞) for max-reduction —
involves:

  - parameterising every [rsum]/[+.] lemma on the monoid laws;
  - reproving [rsum_seq_stride_step] (the per-stride accumulation
    invariant) for a general associative ⊕;
  - threading the identity element [e] through tree-reduction
    initialisation in shared memory;
  - re-checking the [%~] approximation chain (which only holds when
    the device-side and real-arithmetic operations agree, which they
    do exactly for max but only up to floating-point error for sum).

A sound, careful generalisation is well over the 2-hour wall budget
this work was scoped to.  Per the project rules ("if it's >>2h, stop
and report"), this challenge is deferred until either:

  (a) a follow-up PR generalises [reduce_batched_block] to monoids and
      adds an [(fmax, -∞)] instance, or
  (b) a dedicated max-reduction primitive (single-purpose, narrower
      proof surface) is added alongside the sum-reduction one.

Spec sketch (for the future)
----------------------------
The natural shape mirrors [Kuiper.Spec.SumReduceDim.sumreduce_post]:

    let maxreduce_post (rows cols : nat)
                       (sx : chest2 t rows cols)
                       (sout' : lseq t rows) : prop =
      forall (r : nat). r < rows ==>
        (sout' @! r) == seq_max (ematrix_row sx r)

with [seq_max] defined inductively over the row.  Unlike sum,
floating-point [fmax] is associative and bit-equal regardless of
reduction order, so the spec can pin equality (no [%~]) — a strict
strengthening over the sum/mean specs.

Pipeline (when unblocked)
-------------------------
  1. View (B, D, M) row-major as 2-D (B*M, D) via the existing
     [Kuiper.Tensor.Layout.BCMPages] layout (b=B, hw=M, c=D).
  2. One launch of the hypothetical [reduce_batched_block_max] primitive
     with pre_map = id, monoid = (fmax, F32.neg_inf).
  3. Output: length-(B*M) Array1 (l1_forward), reshape to (B, M) on
     the host bridge.
