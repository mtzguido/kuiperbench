L1 #91 cumsum_reverse — Skeptic review
======================================

PyTorch semantics: `torch.cumsum(x.flip(dim), dim).flip(dim)`.

`Kuiper.KB.CumSumReverse.cumsum_reverse_fw_f32` verifies the complete
operation. It applies an inner-coordinate reversal bijection to the input and
output tensor layouts, calls the verified row-block scan through those views,
then restores the original output ownership. The layout transformation is
zero-copy at runtime, but its index mapping and composition with the scan are
inside the proof.

The direct postcondition states that output `(r,i)` approximates the real sum
of the first `D-i` cells of the reversed real input row. There are no new
admits, assumptions, or magic. The Python wrapper has no fallback.

The entry point allocates and returns its owned GPU result. The bridge checks
2-D contiguous row-major fp32, B ≤ 2^21, and the checked uint32 product
bound; selects the input device; makes one extracted call; and wraps the
returned pointer with a `cudaFree` deleter. It performs no flip, arithmetic,
copy, or output allocation.
