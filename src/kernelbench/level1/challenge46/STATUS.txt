STATUS — KernelBench L1 #46 AvgPool3D
=====================================

The challenge has a complete verified count-include-pad implementation.  The
public extracted entry owns the W, H, and D windowed-sum passes, each verified
f32 reciprocal scale, the zero-copy verified layout recasts, all intermediate
allocations and frees, and the final row-major allocation.  Its postcondition
states the exact implementation-order f32 result linked to the original input.

The C++ entrypoint validates the tensor metadata and SizeT/launch refinements,
selects the input CUDA device, calls that entry once, and wraps the returned
cudaMalloc pointer.  No pooling pass, scaling, permutation, allocation, or
multi-kernel orchestration remains on the host side.

The canonical challenge dimensions are (16,32,128,128,256), with kernel 3,
stride 2, and padding 1, producing (16,32,64,64,128).
