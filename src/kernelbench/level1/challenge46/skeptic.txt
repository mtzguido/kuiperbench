SKEPTIC — KernelBench L1 #46 AvgPool3D
======================================

The public extracted entry [avgpool3d_raw_alloc_f32] verifies the complete
three-axis count-include-pad computation.  It allocates intermediates, runs
each WindowReduce1D sum, scales its physical buffer by the corresponding f32
reciprocal, performs proved zero-copy full-layout recasts for the next axis,
frees intermediates, and returns one final allocation.  The C++ bridge only
checks the refinements and invokes this entry once.

The postcondition is tied to the original input and states the exact f32
execution order: windowed W sum and scale, layout recast, windowed H sum and
scale, layout recast, then windowed D sum and scale.  Scaling is described in
physical order through each full layout, so no floating-point algebra or
unproved reordering is used.  The final [l2_bcm_pages] buffer is physically
row-major (B,C,D_out,H_out,W_out).

There is no existential output, host permutation, host allocation, chunking,
or host-side multi-kernel composition.  The bridge's remaining trust is the
ordinary PyTorch/CUDA ABI boundary: tensor metadata/data pointer, checked
integer conversion, wrapping the returned cudaMalloc pointer, and cudaFree in
its deleter.  Non-contiguous input is rejected rather than silently copied.

No challenge-local admit, assume, or magic is added.  The contract deliberately
specifies concrete f32 summation/scaling and makes no numerical-error claim.
