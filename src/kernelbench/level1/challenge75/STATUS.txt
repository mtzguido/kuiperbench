Groups support
--------------
Grouped ConvTranspose is dispatched at the host level: the bridge slices
X along Cin and W along Cin into per-group contiguous slabs, calls the
verified primitive once per group, and writes results back into the
unified Y output.  groups=1 falls through transparently (one
self-allocating verified call + from_blob, no copy).  No proof changes
are required at the kernel level for the grouped variants.  Output
spatial dims now come from the VERIFIED extracted helper [convt_out_dim]
and each per-group output buffer is allocated INSIDE the verified
self-allocating entry [convt2d_general_alloc_f32] (no C++ output-size
formula, no torch::empty for the verified slab).
