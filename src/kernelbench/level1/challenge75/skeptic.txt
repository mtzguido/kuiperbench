skeptic.txt — KernelBench L1 #75
================================

The previous caveat about unverified host-side group slicing, repeated
primitive calls, and output stitching no longer applies.  The public Kuiper
postcondition describes every element of the complete grouped output as the
grouped ConvTranspose2D specification over the original input and weight.

Remaining scope notes:

1. The entry is deliberately specialized to the fixed KernelBench #75
   configuration.  The bridge rejects other shapes, parameters, bias, dtype,
   layout, or device placement rather than falling back to another path.

2. Bias is false in #75.  A zero bias buffer is allocated and initialized by
   a verified Map kernel inside the Kuiper entry, then consumed by the grouped
   kernel and freed before return.

3. As with the existing direct ConvTranspose kernels, kernel descriptor
   setup/teardown and sendability goals are discharged with `solve`; there is
   no admit, assume, or `magic` in the new implementation.

4. Numerical error bounds and special-value guarantees are outside this
   repository's stated verification scope.  The postcondition is exact at the
   floating-operation level.
