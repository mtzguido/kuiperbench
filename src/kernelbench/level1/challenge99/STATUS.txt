Verification techniques used:
- F*-level `exists d_ap d_an` inside `pure` for the loop invariant
  (Pulse couldn't synthesise lseq witnesses at loop entry).
- `triplet_inv_init` vacuous-witness lemma at loop entry.
- `prefix_extend_lemma_ex` for the per-iteration prefix extension
  (uses IndefiniteDescription.indefinite_description_ghost).
- `triplet_final_lemma` discharges `triplet_post` by picking
  d_ap_real := to_real_seq w_ap and d_an_real := to_real_seq w_an.
- `norewrite` on triplet_loss_impl + a 240-rlimit / split-queries
  push-options block on the [triplet_fw_f32] wrapper to satisfy the
  subtype check against [triplet_fw_ty].
